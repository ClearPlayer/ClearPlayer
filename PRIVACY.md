# ClearPlayer Privacy Policy

ClearPlayer ("the App") is a media player that plays back playlists (M3U/M3U8) and EPG data (XMLTV) entered directly by the user. The App does not provide, host, distribute, or index any media content.

## 1. Data We Do NOT Collect
- We do not require a user account, login, or registration.
- We do not operate a server that receives or stores the playlists or EPG URLs you enter: everything is saved **locally on your device**.
- We do not collect or view the media content you play.

## 2. Data We Collect
We use the following third-party services (Google Firebase) for the technical operation of the App:

**Firebase Crashlytics**
- Automatically collects crash reports, including device identifiers, iOS version, device model, and stack traces, solely to identify and fix bugs.

**Firebase Analytics**
- Collects aggregated, anonymized usage data (e.g., features used, app open frequency, interaction events) to help us understand how the App is used and improve it. This data is not linked to your personal identity.

This data is processed by Google in accordance with the [Firebase/Google Privacy Policy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

## 3. How We Use the Data
Data collected through Firebase is used exclusively to:
- Identify and resolve technical issues (crashes, malfunctions)
- Understand feature usage in order to improve the App

We do not sell or share data with third parties for advertising purposes. The App does not contain advertising.

## 4. Data Retention
- Playlists, EPG URLs and settings entered by the user are stored exclusively on the local device and can be deleted at any time by removing the App or through the App's own settings.
- Crashlytics/Analytics data is retained by Google in accordance with Firebase's retention policies.
